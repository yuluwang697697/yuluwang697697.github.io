I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Economics at the University of Ottawa. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Binghamton University (SUNY) in 2026.

My research interests are in empirical industrial organization, applied econometrics, productivity and production functions, and environmental economics. My research focuses on firm productivity and production technology, with particular interests in measuring productivity and technical change, studying input misallocation and market power, and examining how firms respond to technological change, environmental regulation, and other public policies.
