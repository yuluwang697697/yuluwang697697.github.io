I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Economics at the University of Ottawa. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Binghamton University (SUNY) in 2026.

My research interests are in **empirical industrial organization, applied econometrics, productivity and production functions, environmental economics, and labor economics**. My research studies firm productivity and production technology, with a particular interest in how firms respond to technological change, market frictions, institutions, and public policy.

You can find my [CV](/files/Yulu_Wang_CV.pdf) and [Google Scholar profile](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=5wXXVYQAAAAJ&hl=en).

## Research

My current research focuses on three broad areas:

- **Productivity and production functions:** developing empirical methods to measure productivity, technical change, input misallocation, and factor-augmenting technology.
- **Firms, workers, and market power:** studying how wages, unionization, and labor-market institutions affect firms' production decisions and productivity.
- **Environmental and technology policy:** studying how environmental regulation, carbon pricing, and new technologies affect firm behavior and economic performance.

Please see the [Research](/publications/) page for publications and working papers.
