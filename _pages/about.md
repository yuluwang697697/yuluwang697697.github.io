I am a Postdoctoral Fellow in Economics at the University of Ottawa. I received my Ph.D. in Economics from Binghamton University (SUNY) in 2026.

My research areas include empirical industrial organization, applied econometrics, productivity and production economics, and environmental economics. I am particularly interested in topics related to productivity analysis, the identification and estimation of production technology, technical change, market power, and firms’ responses to technological change, environmental regulation, and other public policies.
